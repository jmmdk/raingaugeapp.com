Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short: Rain Gauge has no user accounts. We use the locations you add to fetch weather data for them, we handle subscriptions through Apple, Google and RevenueCat, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Rain Gauge is published by Useful Apps ApS, a company registered in Denmark ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at martin@idearolls.com.

What data we process

  • Locations you add. When you place a rain gauge, the app uses the coordinates of that point. If you choose "Use my location", the app asks your phone for its location once to place the pin. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Purchase information. If you subscribe, your purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive information about your subscription status and purchase history, but never your payment card details.
  • App and device identifiers. A random, anonymous app user ID and device information (such as device model, operating system and app version), used to link your subscription to your device and to keep the app working.
  • Usage data. Basic information about how the app is used (for example which screens are opened), used to fix problems and improve the app.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write to us.

We do not ask for your name, and the app has no login or user account.

Why we use it (and our legal basis)

  • To show rainfall, history and forecasts for your locations – necessary to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • To provide and restore subscriptions – contract.
  • To keep the app stable, secure and to improve it – our legitimate interest in running a working app.
  • To answer your support questions – legitimate interest / contract.

Where your data is stored

Your rain gauges and settings are stored on your own device. We do not run our own server with your locations.

Who we share data with

We only share what is needed with service providers that act on our behalf:

  • Weather data providers (such as OpenWeather and Visual Crossing) receive the coordinates of your rain gauges so they can return rainfall data for those points. They do not receive your name or contact details.
  • Apple and Google process your purchases under their own privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions and receives your anonymous app user ID, device information and purchase history.

Some of these providers are located outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States. Where that is the case, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

How long we keep it

Locations and settings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Purchase records are kept as long as required by accounting law. Support emails are deleted when they are no longer needed to help you.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. To use these rights, email martin@idearolls.com. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.dk) or the authority in your own country.

You can withdraw location access at any time in your phone's settings. Subscriptions are cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Children

Rain Gauge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Important changes will also be announced in the app.
