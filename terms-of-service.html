2. The service

Rain Gauge estimates rainfall for locations you choose, using weather data from third-party providers (models, radar and weather stations). It shows totals, history, forecasts and radar for those locations.

The figures are estimates. They can differ from what a physical rain gauge at the same spot would measure, and data can be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Use Rain Gauge as a guide, not as the only basis for decisions where accuracy is critical, for example in agriculture, construction, insurance or safety matters.
